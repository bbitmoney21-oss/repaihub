## What would flip a no

- FINTRAC registration done, a few real reviews from people who moved a similar amount, and a total fee under about CAD $200 on ₹10 lakh.
- My own Indian bank or a big Canadian bank endorsing or partnering with it, FINTRAC registration completed, a year or more of track record, and a clear written explanation of the Canadian and Indian tax treatment.
- FINTRAC registration actually completed, the named partner bank and CA firm disclosed, and a few months of real track record with transfers of my size.
- Several people in my community group saying they moved money with them without trouble, plus FINTRAC registration completed and a clear explanation of the tax filing on both sides.
- A total cost under about CAD $120 for ten lakh, plus being fully FINTRAC-registered with real reviews from people I know in my community.
- Several people in my community group saying they moved money with them safely, plus FINTRAC registration done and a much lower fee, around 0.5% or less.
- FINTRAC registration done, a track record of a year or more, and my own CA confirming the tax treatment and the Form 145/146 filings are correct.
- Several people in my community group or temple association vouching that they moved money with them and it arrived, plus the FINTRAC registration completed and confirmation that my Indian bank will not need me to visit.
- FINTRAC registration completed, plus the partner bank named and a few reviews from people I know or a community I trust. A lower fee for small amounts would help too.
- A fee under about 0.5% all-in, FINTRAC registration completed, and a few months of real customer track record.
- A fee under about 0.7% with FINTRAC registration done and a track record from real customers. Or if my cousin stopped being able to help.
- Finished FINTRAC registration, a few real reviews from people in my community, and my own accountant saying he has seen it work.
