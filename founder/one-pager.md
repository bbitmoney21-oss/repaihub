# REPAIHUB Safe Passage — one page

**Problem.** An Indian-origin Canadian with savings in an Indian NRO account (rent, matured FDs, property-sale proceeds, inheritance) is told "visit a branch in India". They must also find a Chartered Accountant for Form 146 (ex-15CB) and file Form 145 (ex-15CA). Many wait for the next trip or use informal channels.

**Customer.** Indian-origin PRs and citizens in Canada with NRO balances. The first audience is tax-anxious and busy professionals, plus families with a property sale or inheritance.

**Offer and price.** Done-for-you NRO → Canada repatriation: free CA pre-check, Forms 145/146, transfer through a named RBI-authorised partner bank, live tracking, fee taken only on arrival, redo-free / fee-refund guarantee. **Essential $79 (≤ ₹5L) · Standard $199 (₹5L–₹25L) · Lump-Sum Concierge $499.** Founding 100: Standard at $149.

**Evidence.**
- Simulated panel: original pitch 0/20 buy; redesigned offer 15/69 (22%), an upper bound.
- Competitor gap: no public player offers end-to-end, Canada-based NRO → Canada with paperwork included. Banks serve only their own customers and push the CA work back onto the customer.
- Real-world test: not yet run (Paperwork-Only Pilot planned).

**What the money is for.** Legal and compliance setup ($8K), security test ($3K), bank integration ($5K placeholder), trademark ($1K), launch marketing ($3K).

**The ask.** For a partner bank: a co-branded NRO repatriation corridor for Canadian NRIs, where REPAIHUB brings customers, compliance prep and CA-checked paperwork, and the bank executes. For an accountant: refer clients with NRO money and get a year-end summary for their files.

*Simulated buyers are not customers; projections are not forecasts. Not financial, legal or tax advice.*
