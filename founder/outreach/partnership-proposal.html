Indian-origin residents of Canada hold rupee savings in NRO accounts: rent, matured deposits, pension, property-sale proceeds. Moving that money home today means paperwork, a Chartered Accountant and often a branch visit, and it takes weeks. REPAIHUB prepares each request end to end and hands one Authorised Dealer Category-I partner bank a complete, pre-verified instruction. The bank keeps every AD decision and executes the remittance.

Proposed pilot

  • Scope: up to 50 transfers or ₹5 crore, about 90 days. At the rates above, the bank earns about ₹2.25 – ₹3.5 lakh on the pilot alone.
  • Sequence: agreement → due diligence → integration and testing on a test environment → pilot → review → wider launch.
  • No customer money moves before the agreement is signed.
  • We would need: a partnership owner and an operations contact; fee and FX margin in writing; instruction format (API or file) and payment confirmations; your due-diligence checklist and Form 145/146 verification process.

Compliance and controls

  • FINTRAC MSB Registered, N300002485 (Money Transferring).
  • FEMA: year-to-date tracking against the USD 1M per financial-year NRO limit; a transfer is held, never sent, when the limit cannot be confirmed.
  • PAN verified on every transfer; Form 146 above ₹5 lakh in the tax year; the correct RBI purpose code on every instruction.
  • Data minimisation by design: REPAIHUB stores no identity documents, PAN cards or statements. Only verification results, references and a masked PAN.
  • Full audit trail: every check, reference and status change time-stamped and available to the bank.
  • Sanctions and PEP screening aligned to the partner bank's AML policy before the pilot.

Why REPAIHUB

  • A corridor with no specialist today: mainstream transfer apps serve resident money or India-bound payments. The NRO-to-Canada repatriation, with its tax forms, is still done manually at bank branches.
  • Customer effort cut to minutes: online identity and bank verification, tax forms prepared from verified data, and clear fees shown before the customer confirms.
  • Built both ways: India → Canada repatriation first, with Canada → India remittances on the same platform.
  • Working beta: the full customer journey can be reviewed at repaihub.com (beta preview; no live transfers until our partner bank is in place).
