# Bank partner outreach kit — REPAIHUB

Goal: sign one RBI Authorised Dealer Category-I (AD-I) bank to execute NRO → Canada repatriations for REPAIHUB customers.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `signature.md` | Email signature with the beta link |
| `emails.md` | First email, two follow-ups, and reply templates for every likely answer |
| `demo-brief.md` | Your 30-minute demo script, the questions banks will ask, and what to ask them |
| `tracker.csv` | One row per bank; update after every touch |
| `../one-pager.md` | Attach or paste this when a bank asks for "something in writing" |

## Rules for this outreach
0. **Decision-makers only.** Email named people who can decide (Head of NRI Business, fintech partnerships, international banking/forex, CDO/CBO). Never customer-service, info@, nri@ or grievance inboxes; they send auto-replies. No guessed addresses. See `.claude/rules.md`.
1. **Every email goes out only after Bhagi approves it.** Claude drafts in Gmail; Bhagi clicks Send (or says "send").
2. Send from **founder@repaihub.com**, not a personal address. Banks check the domain.
3. FINTRAC: REPAIHUB Remittance Inc. is registered, MSB No. N300002485 (approved 2026-11-02, expires 2029-07-31; checked on the official FINTRAC registry 2026-11-19). Quote the number exactly. Don't confuse it with "Remittances Hub" (M23799972), a different company.
4. No confidential numbers (bank fees, volumes) in a first email. Those go in the call, under NDA if they ask.
5. Calendar invites go out only after the bank proposes or accepts a time, in the bank's time zone (IST) with Toronto time shown.

## Sequence
Day 0: first email → Day 5: follow-up 1 → Day 12: follow-up 2 (last) → mark "no reply" and move to the next contact.
