# Offers lens
Score: 5 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **The market passes on pain and money.** The buyer has a lump sum sitting in India (sale proceeds, inheritance, matured FDs) and the current route is a branch visit, paper Form 145/146, a CA they have to find, or an informal channel. That is real pain, and the person feeling it has money. Size and growth are unverified: the "$9B" and "1.8M" figures come from the landing page.
- **The buyer is easy to find.** It is one diaspora that gathers in known communities, and the founder is a customer himself and runs a content brand.
- **Dream outcome and time score well.** The promise is "your Indian savings, legally in your Canadian account, in 24–48 h, no trip to India". Today the same job means a trip or a chain of middlemen.
- **Pricing on a percentage is the right instinct.** It ties the fee to the size of the outcome, not to the cost of the transaction.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Likelihood is currently zero.** No AD Category-I bank is signed, and FINTRAC registration is not granted. Until both exist, there is no offer to sell, only a demo.
2. **There is no proof, and the purchase is a high-stakes one.** Someone sending ₹30L through a pre-launch app run by one person needs strong reasons to believe it. There are no completed transfers, no testimonials, no guarantee and no named bank behind the promise. On this lens, likelihood is where offers like this die.
3. **Price confusion pushes it toward a commodity.** The landing page anchors the price at $8–15 flat, while the product charges 1–2% plus $24.99. If the flat price stays, it is a cheap-transfer app competing with Wise and the bank desks. If the percentage stays, the waitlist was sold a different price. The margin is also unknown: the Indian bank's fee and the partner CA's cost per Form 146 are not yet known.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Which AD bank, by when, and at what fee per transfer?
- What does a Form 146 cost you, and is it included above ₹10L?
- What is the guarantee? For example: "lands in 48 h or the fee is refunded", and "paperwork accepted by the bank or we redo it free".
- Will anyone on the waitlist pay a deposit at the percentage price?
- What other problems does the buyer hit that you could stack? For example: working out the tax position before transfer, TDS certificates, a document checklist for property sales, and records for Canadian tax.
- At double the fee, would you still be the obvious choice over a CA plus a branch visit?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. A signed AD bank partner and FINTRAC registration, with the full cost of one transfer worked out.
2. One price, shown the same way everywhere, and value-based rather than "cheapest".
3. A written guarantee, and at least 5–10 completed paid transfers to use as proof.
4. A named, stacked offer (transfer + tax paperwork + CA + tracking + guarantee) that the waitlist has tested with paid deposits.
