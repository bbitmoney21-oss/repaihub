# Email triage rules — founder@repaihub.com

The Founder's Personal Assistant sorts every incoming message into exactly one category. **Nothing is ever sent without the founder's yes on that exact draft (Rules 2–3).**

| Category | Examples | What the assistant does | Founder action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Bank & regulator** | Any AD-I bank, HDFC, ICICI, NRO partner banks, FINTRAC, RBI, CRA, the income-tax department | Same-day summary at the top of the briefing. Drafts a very polite, professional reply (Legal agent reviews). Adds the beta link only if the QA Lead has confirmed it ready. | Approves the draft, or replies himself |
| **B. Partners & vendors** | Zūm Rails, IDfy, Persona, Setu, Razorpay, the CA team | Summary plus a drafted reply. Hands the document requests to Head of Compliance & Partnerships. | Approves |
| **C. Money, contracts, legal** | Invoices, quotes, term sheets, agreements, anything with a fee or a signature | Summary only, with the amount and deadline. Never drafts acceptance. Never pays. | Decides |
| **D. Customers & waitlist** | Questions from the public, beta users | Routes to the Support Agent for a draft. Support Lead reviews. | Approves the draft |
| **E. Internal** | Satwik (CFO), SriHarsha (CA Partner), Deepak (partner) | Summary. Drafts a reply only if asked. | Replies |
| **F. Notifications** | GitHub, Render, Netlify, Supabase, Microsoft, monitoring | Logs anything that signals a failure or a bill to the briefing. Ignores the rest. | None, unless flagged |
| **G. Marketing & cold outreach** | Sales pitches, newsletters, event invitations | Batched once a week as one line each. | Optional |
| **H. Suspicious** | Unexpected attachments, credential requests, payment-change requests, look-alike domains | **Flags it at the top of the briefing. Never clicks, opens, forwards or replies.** | Decides |

## Rules for drafts
1. **Sender:** always founder@repaihub.com. Never any other address (Rule 5).
2. **Bank and government drafts:**
   - very polite and professional;
   - addressed to a named person, never a generic inbox;
   - signed with the founder's approved signature (`founder/outreach/signature.md`).
3. **Every partner draft includes the beta link,** but only once QA has confirmed it is tested end to end (Rule 5).
4. **No draft may:**
   - quote prices while queue item Q-001 (pricing) is open;
   - promise a launch date;
   - describe FINTRAC status as anything other than "Registered, N300002485".
5. **Original wording only.** Never reuse another company's template (Rule 1).
6. **Deadlines:** every draft is queued with a reply-by date. For banks and regulators that is one business day; for others, three.

## Personal mail
Messages that are clearly personal are not summarised, drafted or shown to any agent. They are listed only as "personal — not opened".
