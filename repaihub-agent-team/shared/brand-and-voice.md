# Brand and voice — how REPAIHUB writes

Every word REPAIHUB publishes or sends is original. **Never copy or closely paraphrase another company's copy, templates, taglines or structure (Rule 1).** All external text goes through the approval queue before it leaves (Rules 2–3).

## Who we are, in one line
REPAIHUB brings your own money home from India to Canada, legally, with the paperwork handled.

## Voice by audience

### Banks and government bodies: very polite and professional (Rule 5)
- Formal greeting with the person's name and title. Never use "Hi there".
- Short paragraphs. State who we are, why we are writing, and what we ask, in that order.
- Facts only: "REPAIHUB Remittance Inc., a FINTRAC-registered Money Services Business (N300002485)".
- Never pressure, never criticise another institution, never overstate.
- Include the beta link, once QA has confirmed it ready.
- Close by thanking them for their time and offering a convenient time to speak.
- Sent from founder@repaihub.com only, signed by Venkateswarlu Bhagi, Founder & CEO.

### Customers: clear and plain
- Write for someone reading on a phone, in their second language. Use short sentences and everyday words.
- Explain every term the first time: "Form 146 (formerly 15CB): a Chartered Accountant's certificate that the tax on this money is settled."
- Show the full fee and rate before asking for anything. Never hide costs.
- Be warm, not salesy. Never use fear to sell.
- Never give individual tax or legal advice. Point people to a professional.

### Community and social: helpful first
- Lead with something useful (a rule explained, a common mistake avoided). The product comes second.
- Respect the community's languages and festivals without stereotypes.

## Words we use and avoid
| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| "Registered with FINTRAC, N300002485" | "in process", "licensed bank", "government-approved" |
| "Indicative rate" (until a rate source is contracted) | "live rate" |
| "partner Chartered Accountant" | naming a CA firm without the founder's yes |
| "roughly half the all-in cost of a typical Indian bank repatriation, and comparable to Wise — except Wise cannot move NRO money" | "~4–5% below Indian banks" (checked; not accurate) |
| "Form 145 / Form 146 (formerly 15CA / 15CB)" | only "15CA / 15CB" without the new names |
| "once our partner bank agreement is in place" | promised launch dates |
