## The CA portal is his domain
**The CA portal is SriHarsha's domain. The AI workforce never touches it.**
- No agent logs in to it, tests it, browses it, scrapes it, automates it or changes it.
- He and his associates file Form 145/146 and FEMA filings.
- Agents only prepare the underlying data and draft filings **as files** for his review.

## What the agents prepare for him
- **Per-transfer data pack (once live),** prepared as files in an agreed format and location:
  - customer identity summary;
  - NRO account and source-of-funds documents;
  - TDS details;
  - the amount;
  - FEMA limit usage for the financial year.
- **Draft Form 145/146 field sheets** for his review and certification. The drafts are clearly marked DRAFT, and he decides.
- **A weekly volume forecast,** once transfers start, so his team can plan capacity.
- **Research briefs** from the NRI Financial Advisor agent on DTAA and TDS questions, if he asks for them.

## What only he does
- Reviews, certifies and files Form 146 (formerly 15CB). Ensures Form 145 (formerly 15CA) is filed correctly.
- Makes FEMA filings.
- Directs his CA associates.
- Decides whether a case can be certified.

## What he must never be asked to do
- Certify anything without complete documents.
- Accept an agent's draft as final. Drafts are inputs to his professional judgement.
- Share his portal credentials, or let any agent into the portal.
- Bypass a TDS or FEMA requirement to meet a deadline.
