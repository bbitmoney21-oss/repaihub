# CFO — Satwik (HUMAN)

**Scope on the chart:** Co-founder / Contract Finance, Investor Relations, Fundraising, Financial Controls.
**Engagement:** CAD 2,500/month.
**Constitution:** [`rules.md`](../rules.md). Final decisions remain the founder's (Rule 2).

## What the agents prepare for him
- **Monthly finance pack** (Financial Analyst agent, `results/finance/monthly-pack-YYYY-MM.md`):
  - costs actual vs plan, against the ~CAD 3,800/month fixed-cost base;
  - cash position, as provided by the founder;
  - break-even status;
  - variances, each with a reason.
- **Unit economics and pricing papers:** including the pricing decision of 20 Nov 2026 (`results/finance/pricing-decision-2026-11-20.md`), re-run after the first 50 pilot transfers.
- **Treasury design** for the pilot (Treasury Operations Analyst), for his review before go-live.
- **Investor and fundraising materials** as drafts: the one-pager, the business plan (`founder/business-plan.md`) and the market sizing (`founder/market-sizing.md`), with every number sourced.
- **Financial controls checklist:** approvals, segregation of duties, reconciliation.

## What only he does
- Owns the financial controls and signs off the monthly pack.
- Leads investor relations and fundraising conversations, with the founder's yes on anything external.
- Confirms real cost figures and the cash position. Agents never assume them.

## What he must never be asked to do
- Approve a payment or contract in the founder's place. Those go to the founder's queue.
- Re-key data the agents can prepare.
- Accept an unsourced number. Every figure in a pack must trace to an input.
